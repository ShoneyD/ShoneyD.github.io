      The clue words — open this any time
      Author's purposeWHY the writer wrote it. What they want you to think, feel or do afterwards.
      ThemeThe big idea about life the text keeps pointing at. A full sentence, not one word.
      IronyThe opposite of what you'd expect. He's the one with the gun, but he's the one who isn't free.
      First person / confession"I" telling you something they're ashamed of. It makes you trust them, and judge them.
      ImageryDescription you can see, hear or feel. Slow, painful detail makes you feel something on purpose.
      MetaphorCalling one thing another thing. "A mask", "a puppet".
      EvidenceA quote or fact, plus where it came from, plus what it shows.

      The chain: clue → purpose → theme. Every answer today is one link of that chain.
