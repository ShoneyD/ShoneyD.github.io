      Words for today — open this any time
      Contemporary relevanceWhy an old text still matters today: its themes, characters or problems still show up now.
      ThemeThe big idea about life the play keeps pointing at. A full sentence, not one word.
      Social issueA problem in society: pressure to win, leaders who won't admit mistakes, people pushed into things.
      ScriptA scene written as lines of dialogue, with the speaker's name before each line.
      Stage directionA note in (brackets) telling the actor what to do: (to the audience), (looks at her hands).
      Aside / soliloquyYour character's private thoughts, spoken to the audience. You learned these in Lesson 1 of this unit.
      Purpose statementA short note from the writer: what you were going for, and why.

      The test of your scene: could someone who knows Macbeth recognise it, and could someone who doesn't still feel what it's about?
