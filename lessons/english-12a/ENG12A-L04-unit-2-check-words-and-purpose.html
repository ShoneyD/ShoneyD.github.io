      Every clue word from this unit — open this any time
      DictionThe words a writer picks. Why this word and not a plainer one?
      SyntaxHow sentences are built: a list or a story, long or short, full sentences or fragments.
      Language complexityWhat makes a part hard to read: old words, long sentences, a hidden meaning.
      Figurative languageSaying something not literally true to make a point: metaphor, exaggeration, a strong image.
      IronyThe opposite of what you'd expect.
      First person / confession"I" admitting something. It makes you trust them, and judge them.
      ImageryDescription you can see, hear or feel.
      Author's purposeWHY the writer wrote it.
      ThemeThe big idea about life the text keeps pointing at.
      EvidenceA quote or fact + where it came from + what it shows.
