      Four words for how writing works — open this any time
      DictionThe words a writer picks. Why this word and not a plainer one?
      SyntaxHow the sentences are built: long or short, a list or a story, full sentences or fragments.
      Figurative languageSaying something that isn't literally true to make a point: a comparison, an exaggeration, a strong image.
      Comparing choicesTwo writers, same kind of job, different words. What does each choice change?

      Every answer needs a because. Naming the thing is half. Saying what it does is the other half.
