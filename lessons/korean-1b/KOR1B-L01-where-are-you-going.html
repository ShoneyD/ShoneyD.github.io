      Introduce the Concept

      Part 1 — Where are you going?

      Here's the comic from your textbook, the polite way. A student spots a friend on campus:

      👀 What friends say (the book's version): 어디 가? · 도서관. · 나도! 같이 가자.
      Same conversation, casual. Good to recognize. You'll keep using -요.

      The one new piece is 에. It goes straight after a place and means to. Unlike 은/는 or 을/를, it never changes shape. Batchim or not, it's always 에.

      Part 2 — Coming or going? Ask where the speaker is

      Done looks like

      에, 가요 and 와요 on one card — open this any time
      Place + 에"to" a place. Always 에, batchim or not: 학교에, 집에, 노래방에.
      가다 → 가요 · 갔어요Moving AWAY from where the speaker is standing.
      오다 → 와요 · 왔어요Moving TOWARD where the speaker is standing. 오 + 아요 squish into 와요, like 봐요.
      The trap"I'm coming!" to Mom = 가요! You're leaving the spot you're in.
      Asking어디 가요? (where are you going?) · 어디에 갔어요? (where did you go?)

      In speech, 에 often drops off: 어디 가요? 도서관 가요. Both are fine. Write it in for now.
