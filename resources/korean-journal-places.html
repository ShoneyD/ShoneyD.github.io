To a placeplace + 에 · 학교에 가요 · always 에, batchim or not
Go, went가요 · 갔어요 · moving away from where the speaker is
Come, came와요 · 왔어요 · moving toward where the speaker is (오 + 아요 → 와요)
The trap"I'm coming!" to Mom = 가요! You're leaving where you are.
Asking어디 가요? · 어디에 갔어요? · speech often drops 에 here
