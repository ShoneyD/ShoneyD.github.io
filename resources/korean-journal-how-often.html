Journal Week: How Often 🗓️

Five days, ten minutes each, in your paper notebook. This week every page is about how often: always, sometimes, never.

Same four moves as always, with this week's words already picked for you. Do one day per page. The 🔊 buttons are for checking yourself and for the dictation in move 3; everything you write goes in the notebook.

Dot anything that came slowly. Three dots puts a word at the front of tomorrow's page.

1Copy 5, saying each out loud as you write
2Read the 5 out loud, English covered
3Write 3 from sound only, then check
4One line of your own
