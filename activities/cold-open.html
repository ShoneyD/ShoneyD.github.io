Cold Open

Five lines. Four answers. You can call it whenever you want — but you only get to call it once.

How this works

  1. Read the clue. Decide whether you have enough.
  2. If you do, tap the answer you believe and lock it in. If you don't, ask for the next clue.
  3. Calling it early is worth more. Calling it wrong costs you nothing except finding out where you went wrong — which is the useful part.

The case

What is she hiding?
