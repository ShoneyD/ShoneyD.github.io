The Room

Six people. Three things you get to say. Nobody in here changes their mind for the same reason.

How this works

  1. Read who is in the room and what each of them actually cares about.
  2. Choose what you say. Watch every face — you will move some people and lose others with the same sentence.
  3. The room does not reset between turns. What you say second lands in the room your first choice made.
