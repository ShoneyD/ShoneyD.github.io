🎯 Part 2: Pick the particle

Every particle answers two questions: what job does the word do, and does it end in a consonant?

Job Ends in a consonant
(받침 at the bottom, like 밥) Ends in a vowel
(nothing at the bottom, like 우유)
🏷️ Topic
who/what the sentence is about: "as for…" 밥은저는
🙋 Subject
the answer to 누가 (who?), or the thing that IS good 밥이친구가
🎯 Object
the thing being eaten, drunk or liked 밥을우유를

"I eat ramyeon." Korean says it as me-topic, ramyeon-object, eat.

Done looks like: all 12 sentences filled in. The last one is a ★ challenge.

Sentence starters, if you want them

Not sure if the food ends in a consonant? Look at the last block: if something sits at the bottom, use 을.
