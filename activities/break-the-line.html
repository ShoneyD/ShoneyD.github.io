✂️ Break the Line

Same words, different shape. Cut the lines, add the pauses, and hear what changes.

How this works

  1. Reshape a sentence (about 6 min) · Hear it: rhymes and sounds (about 4 min) · Your turn: write 2–4 lines (about 5 min).
  2. Nothing is timed. Try as many shapes as you like. There's no wrong shape, only different effects.
  3. At the end, one button copies everything for your Canvas submission.

Part 1 — Reshape the sentence

Poets control how fast you read and which words hit hardest with two tools: punctuation (a pause) and line breaks (a pause, plus the last word of a line gets extra weight).

First, pick your sentence.

Tap any gap between two words (the little boxes), then use the buttons to add a mark or a line break. Watch the poem and the rhythm bar change.
