⏳🏹 Time Archer

Every round has a sentence and a time clue. The verbs stand on targets. Read the clock, then shoot the one that fits.

How to shoot

  1. Aim: move the mouse. The sight ⊕ follows it. (Keys: ←↑↓→.)
  2. Draw: press and hold the mouse button (or Space) to pull the string back. Let go to shoot. A full draw flies flatter and faster.
  3. Far targets need you to aim a little above them, because arrows drop. While you draw, a gold ✕ shows where the arrow will land on the target you're aiming at. In later ranges it only shows on the nearer targets.
  4. The targets slide and bob. Aim a little ahead of where yours is going, or shoot as it slows down to turn around. (Teacher: the ↔ button freezes them.)
  5. Missing is fine. Grab another arrow. Only the word you hit counts.
  6. Hit the right word, read why, then press Space or Enter for the next round.
  7. Three ranges. At the end, one button copies everything for Canvas.

Range 1 — Read the Clock

Done looks like: one warm-up shot, then the right verb hit in six rounds: two past, two present, two future.

Range 2 — Helpers Carry the Time

Done looks like: four -ing train rounds, then four helper-trap rounds. The sight starts to sway now, so don’t hold the draw too long.

Range 3 — The Championship

Done looks like: nine mixed rounds (tense names, time-jumpers, run-ons, and verb jobs), then two short answers of your own.
