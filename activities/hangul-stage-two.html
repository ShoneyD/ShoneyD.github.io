Hangul Stage II

Five new letters — except none of them are really new. Every one is a letter you already know with one more stroke on it.

How this works

  1. Finish one part, then press the green button when you're ready for the next.
  2. You can retry as many times as you like — nothing is timed and nothing is scored.
  3. At the end, one button copies everything for your Canvas submission.

Part 1 — Which letter is hiding inside

None of these five letters are new. Each one is a letter you already know, with one more stroke added — and the extra stroke does the same thing every time: it adds a stop, a block, or a push of air.

Click a letter, then click the description of the letter hiding inside it. Say both sounds out loud before you decide — the mouth usually tells you before the shape does.

Part 2 — Pick the sound

You've seen where each letter comes from. Now the other half: what it actually sounds like coming out of your mouth.

Two options each. One is the real sound and one is the near-miss English ears usually reach for first.

Part 3 — Put the new letters to work

Five words that were impossible last week and are readable now. Two of them are people you already listen to.

Click a consonant, then a vowel. Same rule as before — a consonant on its own is not a syllable, so nothing appears until the vowel lands.
