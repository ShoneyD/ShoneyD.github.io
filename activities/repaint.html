Repaint

Same events. Same sentence. You choose the words, and the words choose how it feels.

How this works

  1. Tap any outlined word to swap it for another one. Tap again to keep going.
  2. The bar underneath shows where your sentence currently sits. Move it into the zone you were given.
  3. Retry as much as you like. Nothing is timed and nothing is lost.

Part 1 — Paint it dark

Your job

Nothing frightening happens in this sentence. A light is on, a hallway is there, a door is a certain way. Get the bar all the way into DREAD without adding a single event.

Part 2 — Now paint it calm

Your job

Exact same hallway. Exact same door. Take it all the way to CALM.

Part 3 — Your call

Your job

New sentence. This time you pick the zone you are aiming for, then get there. All three are fair game — but the middle one is the hardest, because it means holding the sentence somewhere it does not want to sit.

Part 4 — Say what you did

Three questions. Short answers are fine — one good sentence beats three vague ones.

Q1. Out of every word you swapped, which single one moved the sentence furthest? Name it, and say what it does to the person reading.

My answer

Q2. In Part 2 you calmed down a sentence without deleting anything that happened. So where was the fear actually coming from in Part 1?

My answer

Q3. Your turn to build one. Write a plain, flat sentence about something completely ordinary — then write it again, repainted, so it feels like something is wrong. Change nothing but the words.

My answer — both versions

Sentence starters — use them if you want

• The word ____ does the most, because it makes the reader ____.
• The fear was not in what happened, it was in ____.
• I swapped ____ for ____, and now the sentence ____.

Don't worry about spelling here. Nobody is marking it.
