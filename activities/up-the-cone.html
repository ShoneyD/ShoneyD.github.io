Up the Cone

H. G. Wells doesn’t just tell you something terrible is coming. He walks you there — step by step, higher and hotter, all the way to the top of a furnace. Rebuild the climb, then find out what the fire is doing to the story.

Part 3 — Swap the setting

Theme is what the story is saying about people in general — something that would still be true about anyone in Horrocks’s spot.

First: which theme does “The Cone” point at? Two of these work — pick the one you can back best.
