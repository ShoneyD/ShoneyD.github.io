    🖼️ Part 2: Nine new words

    Tap a card to flip it, and tap 🔊 to hear the word and a sentence.

    Done looks like: all 9 cards flipped, a look at the ladder, then 9 pictures matched to their word.

    The ladder: how often?

    Above the line, the word works alone: 자주 먹어요. Below it, the word needs 안: 전혀 안 먹어요. Ask about any rung with 얼마나 자주…? (how often…?)

    아직 안 … 었어요not yet아직 안 먹었어요.
    →
    이미 / 벌써 … 었어요already이미 먹었어요. · 벌써 먹었어요?!

    Two "already" words: 이미 just states it. 벌써 sounds surprised: already?!
