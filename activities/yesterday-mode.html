⏪ Part 1: The past-tense machine

먹어요 means "I eat." 먹었어요 means "I ate." The new piece in the middle is 았, 었 or 했, and the verb's last vowel picks which one.

Last vowel ㅏ or ㅗ았어요가 → 갔어요좋 → 좋았어요
Ends in 하했어요공부하 → 공부했어요좋아하 → 좋아했어요
Any other vowel었어요먹 → 먹었어요마시 → 마셨어요

Same vowels squish into one block. 오 + 았 → 왔 and 마시 + 었 → 마셨 squish too. 먹 + 었 has nothing to squish, so it stays 먹었어요.

Done looks like: 12 verbs through the machine, three taps each. The last one is a ★ challenge.

🎯 Part 2: Pick the particle

Every particle answers two questions: what job does the word do, and does it end in a consonant?

Job Ends in a consonant
(받침 at the bottom, like 밥) Ends in a vowel
(nothing at the bottom, like 우유)
🏷️ Topic
who/what the sentence is about: "as for…" 밥은저는
🙋 Subject
the answer to 누가 (who?), or the thing that IS good 밥이친구가
🎯 Object
the thing being eaten, drunk or liked 밥을우유를

"I ate ramyeon." Korean says it as me-topic, ramyeon-object, ate. The past tense changes only the verb; the particles work exactly as before.

Done looks like: all 12 sentences filled in. The last one is a ★ challenge.

🧱 Part 3: Build yesterday

Tap the pieces in order to say the English in Korean. Some pieces are traps: a wrong particle, or the verb in the present tense. Tap a placed piece to send it back. 어제 (yesterday) can go first or right after the topic.

Done looks like: 8 sentences built. The last one is a ★ challenge.

💬 Part 4: Answer it

Three real questions about your yesterday. The voice goes up ↗ at the end, so it's a question. Tap 네 or 아니요 to start your answer, then finish it as a full sentence.

Done looks like: three answers, each starting with 네 or 아니요 and ending in a past-tense verb, plus Q4.

Q4. Your pick. Do one of these:
A. One more thing you did yesterday. (어제 저는 …)
B. Ask Sean a polite question about his yesterday, starting with 혹시. (혹시 어제 …?)

My answer

Sentence starters, if you want them

• 네, 어제 ___을/를 ___었어요.
• 아니요, 어제 ___을/를 안 ___었어요. (안 goes right before the verb)
• 혹시 어제 ___을/를 ___았어요? ↗

Not sure which ending? Check the lanes in Part 1: ㅏ or ㅗ → 았, 하 → 했, anything else → 었.
