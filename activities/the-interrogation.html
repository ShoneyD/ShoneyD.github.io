🙅 Part 2: Two ways to say no

Korean has a short way and a long way to make a verb negative. They mean the same thing.

Both mean "I don't eat." For the past, the ㅆ goes on the last verb: 안 먹었어요 · 먹지 않았어요.

하다 verbs split공부 안 해요공부 + 하다, so 안 slips in between
Own opposites있다 → 없어요
알다 → 몰라요never 안 있어요 or 안 알아요
Can't = 못못 먹어요same spot as 안, but it means you can't

🕵️ Part 3: The interrogation

박 형사 · Detective Park

Detective Park thinks you did it. He's going to ask you negative questions, and that's the trap. Here's how a Korean answer works:

"우유 안 마셨어요?" You didn't drink the milk?

If you didn't네, 안 마셨어요. 네 = "That's right," I didn't.English says: "No, I didn't."
If you did아니요, 마셨어요. 아니요 = "That's wrong," I did.English says: "Yes, I did."

Korean 네 and 아니요 answer the question: is what he said right? English yes and no answer the action: did you do it?
