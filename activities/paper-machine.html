Bottom strip — Works Cited, built for you.

How this works

  1. Research first. Every fact you find goes in your bank with its source attached.
  2. Then you write. Your own facts show up as buttons you tap into each paragraph.
  3. Citations and the Works Cited page are built from your bank. You never format one.
  4. Nothing is timed. You can add to your bank at any point — just reopen Part 1.
