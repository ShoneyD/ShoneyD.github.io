🏹 Bullseye

Every round has a sentence. The words stand on targets. Shoot the one the round asks for.

How to shoot

  1. Aim: move the mouse. The sight ⊕ follows it. (Keys: ←↑↓→.)
  2. Draw: press and hold the mouse button (or Space) to pull the string back. Let go to shoot. A full draw flies flatter and faster.
  3. Far targets need you to aim a little above them, because arrows drop. While you draw, a gold ✕ shows where the arrow will land on the target you're aiming at. In later ranges it only shows on the nearer targets.
  4. The targets slide and bob. Aim a little ahead of where yours is going, or shoot as it slows down to turn around. (Teacher: the ↔ button freezes them.)
  5. Missing is fine. Grab another arrow. Only the word you hit counts.
  6. Five ranges. At the end, one button copies everything for Canvas.

Range 1 — Common or Proper?

Done looks like: one warm-up shot, then the right noun hit in every round.

Range 2 — Point It Back (antecedents)

Done looks like: for each highlighted pronoun, you hit the noun it stands for. Some of them get murky.

Range 3 — Pointers & Clue-Starters

Done looks like: three demonstrative rounds, then three relative rounds.

Range 4 — The -Self Words

Done looks like: three reflexive rounds, then three intensive rounds. Watch the wind flag. It's starting to blow.

Range 5 — The Championship

Done looks like: eight mixed rounds (anything from Ranges 1–4, plus a couple of traps), then two short sentences of your own.
