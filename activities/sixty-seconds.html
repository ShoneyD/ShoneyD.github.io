Sixty Seconds

No teaching, no hints, no reading. Just how much of it is actually in there.

How this works

  1. Pick a deck, then press start. The clock runs for one minute.
  2. Tap the answer. Right, and it's gone. Wrong, and it comes back round.
  3. When the clock stops you'll see what you got and what you missed. You can run it again straight away.

Part 2 — The ones that got away

Two questions, one line each. This bit is short on purpose.

Q1. Pick one you missed. Write down something that will make it stick — a picture, a rhyme, a stupid joke, a link to something you already know. Stupid works best.
