The Dread Dial 🩸

Six lines. Two feelings. Turn the dial to the right one — then argue with me about the last one.

How this works

  1. Three parts. Finish one, then press the green button when you're ready.
  2. Retry as much as you like. Nothing here is timed.
  3. At the end, one button copies your work for Canvas.

TERROR = the dread before. You don't know what's out there yet. Your brain fills the dark in for you.

HORROR = the hit after. You see it, touch it, smell it. No mystery left — just shock and disgust.

Short version: terrified of what might happen, horrified when it did.

Part 1 — Terror or horror?

Six one-line scenes. For each one, tap TERROR or HORROR. Then hit Check.

Part 2 — Name the move

Gothic writers build that dread on purpose. Four lines, four tools. Pick the tool each line is using.

Personification — the setting acts alive.

Hyperbole — turned up way past real.

Foreshadowing — a hint dropped early about what's coming.

Motif — the same image on repeat until it means something.
