Part 1 — Which letter is hiding inside

Korean does something English never does: it takes one letter and makes three out of it. The plain one. The same one with a stroke added — which means more air. And the same one written twice — which means no air at all, said tight.

Four of the five below are the stroke kind. One of them is not. Find it.

Click a letter, then click the description of the letter hiding inside it. Say both sounds out loud before you decide — the mouth usually tells you before the shape does.

Hold a strip of paper in front of your mouth while you do this. It is not a gimmick — it is the actual test, and it is how you will tell these apart for the rest of your life.
