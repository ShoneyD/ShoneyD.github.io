Putting one in Canvas

1. Click Embed on the card, then copy the code for the part you want. 2. In Canvas, click </> in the editor toolbar. 3. Paste, switch back to rich view.

Embed codes are generated from this page's own address, so they're always right for your site. Lesson files built after August 2026 already carry their embeds inside them — copying a section from the lesson gives you the section and its iframe together, so you don't need this page for those.
