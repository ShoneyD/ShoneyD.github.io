      The stand-alone test, the two problems, and the fixes — open this any time
      The stand-alone testCover everything else. Can this piece stand alone and make sense, the way a person would say it and stop? Yes → it's a sentence.
      Run-onTwo stand-alone pieces glued with nothing, or with only a comma. Fix: a period, or , and / , but / , so.
      FragmentA piece that can't stand alone, sitting there with a period. Fix: attach it to the sentence next to it.
      Opening phrasesAfter that, · In the first half, · Before the last round, can't stand alone. They get a comma, never a period.
      Waiting wordswhen, because, after, if, before make a piece wait for more: When the whistle blew, ← waiting… the runners took off.
      Time stamp (L08)Find the helper first: was/were = then, am/is/are = now, will = next.

      Run-ons and fragments are the same question: where does one sentence end?
